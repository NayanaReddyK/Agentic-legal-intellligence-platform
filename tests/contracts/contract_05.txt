DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 18 April 2026 at Bengaluru between:

(1) GREENFIELD FOODS PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 70 Whitefield Road, Bengaluru 560066 (the "Supplier"); and

(2) SOUTHERN TRADERS, a partnership firm having its place of business at 9 Gandhi Bazaar, Mysuru 570001 (the "Distributor").

1. Appointment
1.1 The Supplier appoints the Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Supplier's packaged foods in the State of Karnataka.
1.2 The Distributor accepts the appointment and shall use reasonable efforts to promote the products.

2. Price and Payment
2.1 The Distributor shall pay the Supplier the price stated in the Supplier's price list within forty-five days of delivery.

3. Term and Renewal
3.1 This Agreement begins on 1 May 2026 and continues for five years.
3.2 The Distributor may not terminate this Agreement during the initial five-year term for any reason.
3.3 The Supplier may terminate this Agreement at any time on thirty days' written notice. After the initial term this Agreement renews automatically for further five-year terms unless the Distributor gives notice of non-renewal twelve months before expiry.

4. Minimum Purchase
4.1 If the Distributor purchases less than the minimum quantity set out in Schedule B in any quarter, the Distributor shall pay the Supplier Rs. 10,00,000 for that quarter as liquidated damages, regardless of the Supplier's actual loss.

5. Competing Products During the Term
5.1 While this Agreement is in force, the Distributor shall not distribute any products that compete with the Supplier's products in Karnataka.

6. Post-Termination Restriction
6.1 For two years after this Agreement ends for any reason, the Distributor shall not deal in any competing packaged food products anywhere in India.

7. Indemnity
7.1 Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims to the extent caused by its own breach of this Agreement, up to a maximum of Rs. 25,00,000 for all claims in any contract year.

8. Dispute Resolution
8.1 Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be finally settled by arbitration in Bengaluru under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, by a sole arbitrator appointed by mutual agreement of the parties or, failing agreement within thirty days, by the court under section 11 of that Act. The language of the arbitration shall be English.

9. General
9.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter.
9.2 Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.
