FOUNDERS' AND SHAREHOLDERS' AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 15 March 2026 at Bengaluru between:

(1) NOVACART TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 14 MG Road, Bengaluru 560001 (the "Company"); and

(2) MR. ARJUN MEHTA, residing at 22 Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038 (the "Founder").

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Effective Date" means 1 April 2026.
1.2 "Business" means the operation of an online grocery delivery platform.

2. SHAREHOLDING
2.1 The Founder shall hold 60% of the issued share capital of the Company, subject to vesting over four years from the Effective Date.
2.2 The Founder shall devote full working time to the Business.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION
3.1 The Company may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, without notice or compensation.
3.2 The Founder may terminate this Agreement only by giving the Company six months' prior written notice.

4. NON-COMPETE
4.1 For a period of three years after ceasing to be a shareholder or employee of the Company for any reason, the Founder shall not, anywhere in India, directly or indirectly engage in, be employed by, or hold any interest in any business that competes with the Business.

5. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
5.1 If the Founder breaches any term of this Agreement, the Founder shall pay the Company Rs. 50,00,000 (Rupees Fifty Lakhs) as liquidated damages, irrespective of the actual loss suffered by the Company.

6. INDEMNITY
6.1 The Founder shall indemnify and hold harmless the Company against all losses, liabilities, costs and claims of any nature whatsoever arising out of or in connection with the Business, without any limit on amount or time.

7. PAYMENT
7.1 The Company shall pay the Founder a monthly remuneration of Rs. 1,50,000, payable on or before the 10th day of each month.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
8.1 Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration. The arbitrator shall be appointed by the Company.

9. GENERAL
9.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
9.2 Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first written above.
