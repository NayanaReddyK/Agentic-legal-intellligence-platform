CONSULTANCY AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 9 March 2026 at Chennai between:

(1) VELOCITY MOTORS PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 33 Anna Salai, Chennai 600002 (the "Company"); and

(2) MS. KAVYA SUNDARAM, an independent consultant residing at 12 Adyar, Chennai 600020 (the "Consultant").

1. Engagement
1.1 The Company engages the Consultant to advise on supply chain optimisation as described in Schedule A.
1.2 The Consultant is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Company.

2. Fees and Payment
2.1 The Company shall pay the Consultant Rs. 2,50,000 per month within thirty days of receiving a correct invoice.

3. Term and Termination
3.1 This Agreement continues for twelve months from 1 April 2026.
3.2 The Consultant may terminate this Agreement by giving the Company ninety days' written notice.
3.3 The Company may terminate this Agreement immediately at any time without reason, and shall not owe the Consultant any fees for work in progress at the time of termination.

4. Confidentiality
4.1 The Consultant shall keep confidential all non-public information of the Company and shall use it only for the purposes of this Agreement.
4.2 This obligation survives for three years after this Agreement ends, except for information that is public through no fault of the Consultant or required to be disclosed by law.

5. Non-Solicitation During the Term
5.1 While this Agreement is in force, the Consultant shall not solicit any customer of the Company for the Consultant's own business.

6. Post-Term Restriction
6.1 For three years after this Agreement ends, the Consultant shall not provide services to any customer of the Company or to any business in the automotive sector anywhere in India.

7. Delay Charges
7.1 If the Consultant delivers any report later than the agreed date, the Consultant shall forfeit all unpaid fees and pay the Company Rs. 2,00,000 for each week of delay, regardless of any loss suffered by the Company.

8. Indemnity
8.1 The Consultant shall indemnify the Company against third-party claims that the deliverables infringe intellectual property rights, up to a maximum equal to the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim arose.

9. Dispute Resolution
9.1 Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be finally settled by arbitration in Chennai under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The sole arbitrator shall be appointed by the Managing Director of the Company.

10. Governing Law
10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and subject to clause 9 the courts at Chennai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

11. General
11.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter.
11.2 Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.
